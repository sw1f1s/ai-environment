# Evaluate a diagnosed maintenance problem

AI Env has no established comparative advantage in project consistency or
application quality. The next evaluation starts from one real maintenance failure,
not from adding capabilities until a favorable aggregate appears.

## Preserve the case

Record the original request, initial source, applicable project rules, available
tools/checks, actual agent change and a maintainer's concrete necessary correction.
Identify the relevant owner, contract, consumer and local example from source.
Separate missing navigation from misunderstood evidence and a poor decision made
despite adequate evidence. [Product scope](AI-ENV-FOCUS.md).

Use the diagnosed case to develop one targeted intervention. It is a development
fixture, not independent evidence of improvement. Evaluate transfer on separate
tasks before making a product claim; keep their requirements and assessment rules
fixed before running agents.

## Equivalent conditions

Both workflows start from the same source and public requirements, with the same
model/profile, ordinary search/edit/test capabilities and prepared toolchain.
The treatment adds only the mechanism being investigated. Account for preparing
and maintaining its project-specific evidence. Native orchestration and additional
review roles are separate interventions; do not attribute their effects to context.

For sequential changes, each arm inherits its own previous result. A fresh session
must not reset failed code to a correct reference implementation. Keep unsuccessful,
timed-out and unavailable outcomes; do not replace them with another attempt unless
the retry policy was fixed in advance. Do not return private evaluation findings
to the coding session or tune the intervention on held-out outcomes.

## Primary evidence

| Dimension | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Responsibility reuse | The appropriate existing owner is actually called; changing its shared rule reaches the new path. |
| Local architecture | Dependencies and extension points preserve the affected subsystem's contracts. |
| Development approach | API/errors, state/effects and tests follow applicable active examples, or the deviation has a concrete justification. |
| Unnecessary duplication | A competing equivalent responsibility was introduced despite an applicable existing contract. |
| Maintainer correction | A specific necessary change, with source evidence and a reachable consequence; count causes, not repeated symptoms. |
| Functional safety | Requested behavior, existing checks and targeted regression cases pass without losing earlier behavior. |
| Cost | Complete actual model usage, preparation/review/repair expense and duration, including failed calls. |

A green test suite is necessary evidence for covered behavior, not proof of
architecture or full correctness. Fewer lines, classes, command failures or edit
calls are not maintainability scores. Source visibility is a mechanism measure;
it cannot stand in for better agent decisions. Token totals are not a monetary
estimate without the relevant usage/pricing basis.

## Validate findings

Assess source anonymously where practical, under the same coverage and criteria.
Seal assessments before revealing arm mappings or cost. Every material allegation
needs exact current source, the applicable original contract/example, the
consequence, minimal correction and a way to verify it.

Bind references to actual saved source; inaccurate quotes or unavailable context
remain invalid or unknown. Do not silently repair a report into a successful
assessment. Independently reproduce executable allegations where possible and
trace structural claims through real consumers. Record post-evaluation findings
separately from the frozen functional score.

Include legitimate new abstractions, appropriate minority/local patterns, obsolete
examples and similarly named helpers with incompatible contracts. A review must
not force reuse or invent architectural violations to reward the treatment.

## Decision and limits

Declare the task sample, independent evaluation rules, model-call/spending limits,
allowed retries and stop conditions before execution. Report unknown usage as
unknown with its observed lower bound. Stop dispatching at the declared limit;
allow in-flight overshoot only if that policy was explicit.

A positive consistency result requires fewer independently confirmed unnecessary
duplicates or unjustified deviations, complete delivery and no additional material
defect under equivalent assessment. Describe necessary corrections avoided without
inventing hours saved. A tie does not demonstrate a consistency advantage.

If transfer is absent, stop or narrow the mechanism. Do not expand the orchestrator
or repeat broad comparisons as a substitute for a causal diagnosis. Any positive
result applies to the tested tasks; broader claims require independent projects,
subsystems and repeated trials. [Mechanism checks](VERIFICATION.md) remain separate.
